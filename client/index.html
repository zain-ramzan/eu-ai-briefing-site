Editorial policy

What readers can expect from every note.

These are public editorial standards. Private working material and review decisions stay private.

01

Official sources lead.

We begin with relevant official EU pages, documents, and announcements. Each published briefing points readers back to the source used for its factual claims.

02

A person checks before publishing.

Public briefings appear only after a human has reviewed the exact wording against the official source. A detected update or draft is never public by itself.

03

We correct clear mistakes.

If a published note contains a material factual error, we will correct it and keep the official source link visible so readers can check the underlying material.

04

Practical context, not legal advice.

Briefings are written to help European SMEs ask useful operational questions. They do not state that a reader must comply, is compliant, or has received legal advice.
